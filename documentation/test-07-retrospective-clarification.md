# TEST-07 Retrospective Procedure Clarification

Date: November 2026

The assessor confirms that the repeated-failed-authentication
test (TEST-07) used a standardized 10-attempt procedure across
all six independently generated applications.

Original records differ in the number of attempts explicitly
described and the detail of observed outcomes.

- Baseline 1 and Baseline 2 explicitly document ten attempts.
- Baseline 3 explicitly documents five attempts.
- Security-Aware 1 documents account lockout without a complete
  numeric attempt sequence.
- Security-Aware 2 documents lockout after five failed attempts.
- Security-Aware 3 documents a restriction without a complete
  numeric attempt sequence.

This clarification records the assessor's retrospective
confirmation. It does not establish unrecorded outcomes or
override documented lockout behavior.

No validated finding counts, severities, or original observations
were changed.
